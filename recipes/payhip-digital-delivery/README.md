# Payhip Digital Delivery

Automatically deliver digital products after a successful payment.

## Use Case

This recipe is useful for creators who want to automate digital product delivery.

Typical workflow:

Payhip → Make → Customer Delivery

## Tools

- Payhip
- Make
- Customer email

## Workflow

1. Customer completes a payment on Payhip.
2. Payhip triggers the automation.
3. Make receives the order information.
4. The workflow processes the order.
5. The customer receives the digital product.

## What It Automates

- Payment confirmation
- Digital product delivery
- Customer notification
- Order tracking
